Questions

What do I get for $149?

An attach trace — we attempt Claude, Cursor, Desktop, and Grok against your server and write which step worked or died in each client, with real responses. Screenshots are evidence, not the product.

Why not just run the free checker?

diagnose.py reads metadata. It never hits registration, never exchanges a code, never calls tools/list. Four real clients do things metadata cannot predict — that is what you are buying.

What if my server already fails the checker?

Buy the trace. Failures and unknowns are the intended buyers. The checker is a scoping step, not a gate that blocks payment.

What is a fix round?

After the trace, we quote a separate fix (typically $600–$1,500). We change what is needed so Connectors attach; you review and merge. Not included in $149.

Do you change my server in the $149 round?

No. The trace round is read-only against your origin from our accounts. Code changes are the fix round.

What if you cannot tell me why it failed?

You get your money back. That is on the page, not a private note.

How do I pay?

On this site, through Stripe. Installing the checker from GitHub is free. This is not billed through Cursor.
